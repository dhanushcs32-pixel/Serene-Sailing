## Take to the High Seas

**Serene Sailing** transforms Minecraft's oceans into living, breathing waters of adventure. Build custom sailing ships block by block, raise canvas sails to harness the winds, steer through rolling ocean waves, and brave towering swells and raging tempest storms.

Whether you're exploring tranquil coastlines on a wooden sloop or captaining a multi-masted galleon through a violent gale, Serene Sailing brings the romance and challenge of the Age of Sail to life.

## Features

### ⛵ Build & Sail Real Ships
- **Block-by-Block Construction:** Design your dream vessel using your favorite building blocks, wood types, and deck fixtures.
- **Full Physics Sailing:** Once assembled, your creation becomes a living, buoyant ship that floats, bobs, pitches, and rolls naturally with the water.
- **Watertight Bilges & Cabins:** Walk freely below deck. Fully enclosed hulls create dry air pockets beneath the waterline so cabins, galleys, and cargo holds stay dry without ocean water leaking into interior spaces.

### 🌊 Dynamic Ocean Waves & Rolling Swells
- **3D Ocean Swells:** The sea is no longer a static, flat surface. Ocean water undulates with rolling swells and wind-driven wave crests.
- **Responsive Ship Motion:** Hulls climb wave crests, plunge into troughs, and heel gracefully in response to turning and ocean conditions.
- **Coastal Breakers & Spray:** Foaming surf rolls toward beaches and shoals, accompanied by sea spray and bow wakes as your ship cuts through the water.

### 💨 Realistic Wind & Sail Aerodynamics
- **Catch the Wind:** Wind direction and velocity directly dictate your vessel's speed. Catch crosswinds on a swift beam reach, run before the wind, or tack into headwinds.
- **Mind the Dead Zone ("In Irons"):** Pointing your bow directly into the wind will stall your sails and bleed speed. Master the rudder and helm to fall off the wind and keep your vessel moving.
- **Multiple Sail Reefing States:** Shift your canvas sails between **Full Sail** (maximum speed in fair winds), **Reefed/Half Sail** (safe maneuvering and heavy air), and **Furled/Rolled** (docking and anchoring).

### 🌪️ Storms, Gales & Tempest Survival
- **Heavy Weather:** In overcast and rainy conditions, ocean waves grow taller and wind gusts pick up.
- **Weather2 Integration:** When used alongside Weather2, your ship interacts directly with real localized storms, tropical cyclones, and tornadoes. Navigate mountainous storm seas, trim your sails against hurricane-force squalls, or fight the helm to escape a waterspout!

## Deck Equipment & Instruments

Serene Sailing adds finely detailed, Age-of-Sail and Create-styled mechanical instruments and fixtures to outfit your vessel:

### ⚓ Ship's Wheel (Helm Station)
The centerpiece of any ship's quarterdeck.
- **Interactive Steering:** Grab the wheel and steer smoothly to port or starboard.
- **Turned Spokes & King Spoke:** An ornate 8-spoke wheel mounted on a carved binnacle pedestal with an authentic brass-capped King Spoke to indicate center rudder at a glance.
- **Rudder Integration:** Commands your underwater rudders to turn the vessel with authentic hydrodynamic response.

### 🚩 Wind Vane
A nautical masthead and deck weathercock.
- **Real-Time Wind Direction:** Features a balanced brass pointer, diamond arrowhead, and split-swallowtail pennant that swings to indicate wind direction relative to your ship.
- **Cardinal Crossbars:** Fixed directional markers help you quickly read your angle to the wind for optimal sail trim.

### ⏱️ Taffrail Log
A stern-mounted nautical speedometer.
- **Speed Telemetry:** Trails behind the vessel's transom to measure speed through the water.
- **Knots Readout & Redstone Output:** Displays your speed in nautical knots and outputs an analog redstone comparator signal (0–15) to automate shipboard mechanisms, sound warning bells, or trigger alarms based on speed.

### ⚓ Capstan & Anchor Mooring
- **Secure Mooring:** Drop anchor to lock your ship firmly in place against currents and wind drift.
- **Visual Anchor Chain:** Deploys a heavy iron chain descending to the ocean seabed.

## Sailing Tips for New Captains

1. **Trimming for Speed:** Ships sail fastest on a **Beam Reach** (wind coming across the side of the hull) or a **Broad Reach**. Running straight downwind is easy, but reaching will often give you superior speed!
2. **Escaping "In Irons":** If your bow points straight into the wind, forward drive will drop to zero and aerodynamic drag will push your vessel backward (sternway). Turn the helm while in sternway to swing the bow across the wind until the sails fill again.
3. **Rough Seas & Squalls:** When the sky darkens and waves rise, reef your sails to half or furl them entirely to prevent wild heeling and maintain control.
4. **Docking:** Drop to furled sails well before reaching piers or docks, letting natural hydrodynamic drag glide you smoothly into port. Drop anchor with the capstan to moor securely.

## Getting Started

### Installation
1. Install **Minecraft 1.21.1** with **NeoForge**.
2. Install the required core dependency: **Aeronautics / Sable**.
3. *(Optional)* Install **Weather, Storms & Tornadoes (Weather2)** for dynamic storm systems, wind gusts, and tornadoes.
4. Drop `serenesailing-1.0.0.jar` into your `.minecraft/mods` directory.

### Quick Start Guide
1. **Lay Your Keel:** Build a ship hull in the water using solid blocks (wood planks, logs, decks). Ensure your hull forms an enclosed shape for watertight cabins.
2. **Mount Rigging:** Place **Canvas Sails** aloft on yardarms and masts. Install a **Ship's Wheel (Helm)** on deck and one or more **Rudders** underwater at the stern.
3. **Add Instruments:** Install a **Wind Vane** atop the mast or cabin roof and a **Taffrail Log** at the stern transom.
4. **Assemble & Embark:** Assemble your vessel using the Physics Assembler. Right-click the Helm to take the wheel, click your sails to unfurl canvas, and set sail into the horizon!
